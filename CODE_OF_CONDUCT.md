# Code of conduct

This applies to everyone who takes part in this project: in issues, pull
requests, discussions, and any other place the project uses.

## What is expected

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume the other person means well, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Stay on the subject of the issue or pull request.
- Give enough detail that others can help, and be patient with those who
  are learning.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what goes into the project, and may say
  no.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory remarks about anyone's background, identity or beliefs.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Asking for, or posting, ways to get around paywalls, DRM, or other access
  controls, or links to material shared without the right to share it.
- Spam and advertising.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block
people who do not keep to this. For a first, minor problem the usual step is
a reminder; repeated or serious problems lead to a block without warning.
