## What YTDL is

YTDL is a graphical front end for [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp),
an independent open-source command-line program. YTDL builds a command, runs
yt-dlp on your Mac, and shows its progress. It does not host, index, stream,
or redistribute any media, and it has no servers of its own.

## Files on your Mac

YTDL writes files to the folder you choose and can delete files it created.
Cancelling a download removes that download's partial files by default, and
"Download Again" moves the earlier copy of a file to the Trash. Keep backups
of anything you cannot afford to lose. See the
[user guide](docs/user-guide.md) for exactly what is deleted and when.
